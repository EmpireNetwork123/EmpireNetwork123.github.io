I received my M.Eng. in Cybersecurity from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), China, under the supervision of Professor Songfeng Lu. My master's research introduced a decentralized, privacy-preserving ransomware-detection framework that learns real-world attacker behavior across multiple participants without sharing sensitive threat telemetry; this architecture lets firms and agencies share and integrate threat data without incurring privacy penalties. With its rich TTP-enriched dataset, RS-FEDRAD achieved strong performance under both centralized and federated architectures. We integrated LIME and SHAP to highlight the influence of the rich behavioral telemetry (TTPs, API, DLL and Mutual Exclusions) on the model's decision-making process.

Research

Behavioral Threat Detection

Learning how attacks unfold over time from system and network telemetry, so that detectors recognize malicious intent, including previously unseen attacks, and can explain their decisions.

  • ATT&CK-informed modeling of malware and intrusion behavior
  • Temporal deep learning for ransomware and malware detection
  • Explainable and adversarially robust security models

Trustworthy Federated and Distributed Security

Enabling organizations and devices to defend collaboratively without sharing sensitive data, while remaining robust to unreliable or malicious participants.

  • Privacy-preserving federated threat detection
  • Trust assessment and poisoning resilience under non-IID data
  • Zero Trust architectures for distributed and cyber-physical systems
